How Compound Interest Accelerates Wealth

Compound interest occurs when your investment earnings generate their own earnings over time. Unlike simple interest, which is calculated only on your initial principal, compounding reinvests your dividends and returns every month or year.

Consistent monthly contributions combined with time in the market create a snowball effect. Historically, the US S&P 500 index has delivered an average annual return of roughly 9% to 10% over long multi-decade horizons.
